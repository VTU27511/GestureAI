Gemini AI Configuration

GATEway AI uses Google's state-of-the-art **Gemini 1.5/2.0 API** to provide deep reasoning, concept tutoring, and custom question solving.

Your key is saved locally in your browser cache and is never sent to any external server other than Google's secure APIs.

How to get a free API Key:

  1. Go to the Google AI Studio .
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Click "Get API Key" in the top-left menu.
  4. Click "Create API Key" and copy it.
  5. Paste the key into the input field above.
